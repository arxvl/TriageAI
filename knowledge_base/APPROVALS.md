# Clinical Content Approval Log

**Scope note.** For this academic project, external veterinary sign-off has been waived. The `can_approve_kb` approval workflow remains a system feature (BR-04, FR-52), and the team holds the approving account.

Content must be accepted on the basis of **source traceability and independent team verification**: clinical claims must be traceable to published, veterinarian-authored sources, and a second team member must verify that each entry, rule, and phrase accurately reflects its source. Procedural workflow suggestions must be identified as team-authored rather than misrepresented as published clinical recommendations.

**This system has not been clinically validated.** Team verification is not equivalent to veterinary approval or clinical validation. No entry should be marked accepted until its source has been checked and the second reviewer has completed verification.

If a licensed veterinarian reviews any item later, add a new row with Decision = `Veterinarian approved`, including their name and licence number. That row supersedes the team verification for that item.

## Approval completion checklist

- [ ] Confirm each source URL, title, author, and relevant clinical claim.
- [ ] Confirm red-flag identifiers and category mappings exist in the project's configuration.
- [ ] Have a second team member verify each entry and rule against the cited source.
- [ ] Record actual names and dates after verification.
- [ ] Run the action validator and record its real result.
- [ ] Confirm the M7 vignette set and rubric version before labelling.
- [ ] Calculate Cohen's kappa from the completed independent labels.
- [ ] Keep the system's unvalidated status explicit in project documentation.
